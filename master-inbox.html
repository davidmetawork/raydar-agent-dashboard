Supported search syntax

The store reads from:, to:, after:, before:, in:, mailbox:, label: and has:attachment. Dates are YYYY-MM-DD. Anything else, including subject:, filename:, cc: and is:unread, is searched as ordinary message text; when that happens the search box says so underneath.
